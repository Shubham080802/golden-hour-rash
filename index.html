          The punishment is deleted, the violence isn't

          Road Rash's catharsis was the swing; its stress was the loss — you fell off, jogged back, watched the pack vanish. Here a wipeout costs you speed and your combo, never the run. You remount instantly and the pack rubber-bands back to you.

          That single inversion is the product.

          A song, not a clock

          The run is exactly as long as the track playing, and the HUD shows a progress bar, not a countdown. Same information, opposite feeling — one says "time is running out", the other says "the song is playing".

          Zen removes even that: no rivals, no meter, no score. Same world, opened at 11pm.

          Competition that can't stress you

          Daily gives every player the same seeded road and the same traffic, then a shared board. The competition is asynchronous — you're chasing a number, never another person in real time, and never on the clock.

          It splits in two: Guided hands you a road map and corner calls, Blind gives you nothing. Separate boards, so difficulty is a choice rather than a handicap.

          No live lobbies, no ranked decay, unlimited attempts. Your posted score only moves up.
