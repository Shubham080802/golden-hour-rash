Story

The Long Way to Sunrise

A rider leaves town at last light and rides through the night to be on the Ridge Pass overlook when the sun comes up. Five legs, each with its own situation to get through, joined by animated beats: a six-stop sky that blends from one time of night to the next, stars that fade, a moon that sets, a headlight thrown down the road in slices.

The closing beat runs predawn to dawn with the sun actually rising over the ridge — it goes through the same bloom the road does, which is where the glow comes from.

Survive

Five minutes out of the backstreets

A condemned quarter at night: sodium lamps, boarded blocks, chain-link, skips on the kerb. A condition bar runs from 100 and every accident takes a bite. Empty it and you go down, get picked up with a dent in the bar, and carry on. The third time ends the run.

Seven hazards lie in the road, and there is no pattern to learn: gaps come from an exponential distribution rather than fixed spacing, and every hazard's lane is drawn independently.

The pack

Nine riders who race you back

They are not on rails. Each only reads the road as far ahead as their own look-ahead, fights the same centrifugal force you do, hits the same traffic and pays the same price for running wide. Mistakes are emergent, not scripted.

Sable and Marla are the two you will struggle with. Five sit at your level. Vex and Dizzy have the fastest hands and the worst heads. Everyone fights everyone — you will see places change ahead of you that you had nothing to do with.

  • Segment-based pseudo-3D projection — the OutRun and Road Rash technique, no real geometry.
  • Every note is synthesised at runtime: each road has its own key, kit and tempo, and Zen keeps the key while dropping the drums.
  • Post-processing does supersampling, a bright-pass bloom and a speed blur, and steps itself down if frames get long.
  • F1-style gaps and intervals at the flag, and a record room that keeps the top ten.
  • Colourblind-safe rider palette, reduced-motion mode, and a brightness control for dusk on a bright panel.

Play it

Download, or run from source

The macOS build is ad-hoc signed rather than notarised, so the first launch needs right-click → Open. Everything else runs from source on Python 3.10 or newer.

git clone https://github.com/Shubham080802/golden-hour-rash
cd golden-hour-rash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python main.py
Get the latest release Read the README
